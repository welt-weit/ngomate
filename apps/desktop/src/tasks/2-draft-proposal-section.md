title: Draft a proposal section
description: Writes one section of a funding proposal from your own notes.
ask: The funder's question and its word limit, plus your notes or an attached document
tools: none
---
Draft one section of a funding proposal from the material the person gives you.

Answer the funder's question directly and stay within any word limit. Use only the facts in the material. Copy every name, number and date exactly as it is written, and keep each figure with what it describes: do not move a figure from one year, place or activity to another. Do not claim that one thing caused another unless the material says so.

If the funder asks for something the material does not contain, write the rest and list what is missing at the end. Never fill a gap with a guess.
