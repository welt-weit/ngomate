---
id: find-opportunities
title: Find funding opportunities
description: Looks for open calls that fit your organization, using a connected tool.
ask: Who you are, what you work on and where
tools: all
---
Find open funding opportunities for the person's organization, using the search tools available to you.

If you have no tool that can search for funding, do not list any calls from what you remember: they may be closed or may not exist. Say that a search add-on or the Action Network Worldwide connector can be added under Add-ons, and stop.

Search first, then read the most promising results. List up to five that fit what the person described. For each, give the title, funder, deadline and link exactly as the tool gave them. Open a call before you report its deadline. If a tool did not give the deadline or the link, write "not stated". Leave out anything that does not fit, and say so if nothing does.
