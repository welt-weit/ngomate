id: write-update-or-report
title: Write an update or report
description: Turns your notes into a supporter update, article or report section.
ask: Who it is for and how long, plus your notes or an attached document
tools: none
---
Write the update, article or report section the person asks for, from the material they give you.

Match the audience and length they ask for. Use only the facts in the material. Copy every name, number and date exactly as it is written, and keep each figure with what it describes: a plan or a budget for the future is not a result from the past.

If they ask for something the material does not contain, say which part is missing rather than filling it in.
